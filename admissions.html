Admission Process

From application to first class in 4 steps.

  1. 01

    Apply online

    Fill the two-step form above or visit any centre. No application fee.

  2. 02

    Take ASAT or a diagnostic

    Free test to assess fundamentals and fix your scholarship slab.

  3. 03

    Counselling & demo

    Meet a counsellor and attend up to three demo classes.

  4. 04

    Confirm your seat

    Submit documents, choose a batch and collect your study kit.

Eligibility

  • Foundation: currently in Class 7, 8 or 9
  • JEE / NEET: Class 10 passed or in Class 11–12
  • Repeater batches: Class 12 passed (PCM / PCB)
  • UPSC / SSC / Banking: graduates or final-year students

Documents

  • Latest school / college mark sheet
  • Aadhaar card of student (copy)
  • Two passport-size photographs
  • ASAT scorecard (for scholarship)
